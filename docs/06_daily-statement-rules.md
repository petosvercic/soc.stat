# 06 — Daily Statement Rules (Phase 2)

## Purpose
Define how the "statement of the day" stays aligned with FOUNDATION language laws.

## Core Format Rules
- **Length:** 8–16 words.
- **Form:** single sentence.
- **Tense:** present-focused, today-oriented.
- **Voice:** observational, soft, non-authoritative.
- **Addressing:** no direct address (no "you", no imperative).

## Tone Rules
- Neutral to gently reflective.
- No hype, no coaching, no correction.
- Calm enough to allow silent exit.

## Forbidden Terms / Patterns
- Diagnostic vocabulary (e.g., disorder, trauma diagnosis, pathology labels).
- Prescriptive commands (e.g., must, should, fix this, do this now).
- Value judgment labels (e.g., weak, broken, superior, better than others).
- Identity permanence claims (e.g., "this is who someone is").

## Allowed Metaphor Space
- Weather (rain, fog, wind, clear sky, dusk light).
- Natural movement (tide, current, still water, slow turning).
- Light/shadow contrast in non-dramatic form.

## Review Checklist
A statement passes only if:
1. It can be read as "today" and not identity.
2. It gives orientation without instruction.
3. It avoids diagnosis and judgment.
4. It sounds human, not personal or intrusive.
5. It allows stopping without penalty.

## Example Statements — "Rainy Day" Vibe (10)
1. Today carries a soft rain, with thoughts moving at walking pace.
2. A grey tone stays present today, steady rather than heavy.
3. Today feels like light drizzle, quiet and slightly distant.
4. The day holds damp air, with calm sitting close to the surface.
5. A rainy rhythm shapes today, slow, clear, and unforced.
6. Today keeps a muted sky, leaving space between each impulse.
7. Fine rain marks the day, with emotion arriving in small waves.
8. The mood today resembles wet pavement, reflective without urgency.
9. Cloud cover stays gentle today, with moments of softer light.
10. Today moves like rain on glass, steady, subtle, and contained.
